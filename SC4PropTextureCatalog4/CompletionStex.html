STEX Package Completion Tracker

This page tracks the STEX packages tracked in this catalog. Because the contents of this catalog uses the sc4pac cache for its data, it may also be used to indicate an item's inclusion in sc4pac. Each square is one STEX upload. Filled ids have been completed and are indexed. Deleted, hidden, or otherwise non-functioning STEX ids are not yet factored into this chart.
